The AI Assistant is sold as a **purchasable add-on**, separate from your plan
tier — it doesn't require an upgrade by itself, but your organization does
need to be on the right plan to buy it.

## How to purchase

A `super_admin` enables the add-on from **Settings → Billing**. It's added as a
line item on your existing subscription — one invoice, prorated automatically,
no separate checkout.

  canceled, the add-on turns off automatically.

## Data-connection: ENTERPRISE custom only

Connecting the AI Assistant to your own **HTTP endpoints or a database**
(the "data tools" that let it answer with your real data) is an
**ENTERPRISE-only, custom-onboarding feature** — it is not self-serve, even for
ENTERPRISE organizations by default.

- Requires the ENTERPRISE plan **and** the AI Assistant add-on active.
- Requires a manual onboarding step from our side to flag your organization
  for custom data-connection access.
- [Contact our sales team](/contact) to get set up.

Without data-connection access, the AI Assistant can still be enabled, but it
will only be able to answer from context you provide during onboarding — it
won't have live tools to call.

## Usage quota

AI Assistant replies count against your organization's **monthly AI usage
quota**, shared with the operator-assist AI features (Generate Reply / Improve
Message). See [Plans & Pricing](/v1/plans) for the caps that apply to your
plan; ENTERPRISE organizations can have a custom cap configured.
